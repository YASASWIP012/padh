# Running a pilot day

A one-page guide for the adult running sessions at school. One laptop or phone is enough.

## Before the first day
1. Get a short written OK from the head teacher.
2. Print the consent form (https://padh.padh-read.workers.dev/#/consent), one per child. A parent signs it and the school keeps it.
3. On the device, open Teachers, then "Start a new class". Choose a 6-digit PIN and write the class code and PIN on paper.
4. Pick the second adult who will do the blind checks. It must be someone other than you, ideally a school teacher.

## Each child, first check (about 3 minutes)
1. Teachers, then "Add a child". Use a nickname such as "Blue Kite", never a full name. Tick consent and type the name of the adult who collected the form.
2. Padh places the child in a group: "Practice now" or "Storybook first". Note it down.
3. Press "First check" and type your name. Ask the child: "Would you like to read for me?" Stop if they say no or seem upset.
4. Sit somewhere quiet. Press the orange button, let the child read, press it again. Don't help them read.
5. Press "Show the result (adults)" only after the child has left.

## Every school day until the second check
- "Practice now" children: press "Practise", do the 10-minute plan together, then "+10 min practice".
- "Storybook first" children: read any storybook aloud together for 10 minutes, then "+10 min storybook".

## Blind checks (second adult, any day)
- Open the class and press "Check recordings". Listen, mark every word, type the reading time in seconds, and save. About 5 minutes each.

## Second check (opens 7 days after each child's first)
- Press "Second check" and run it the same way as the first.

Results appear on https://padh.padh-read.workers.dev/#/evidence without anything else to do.
